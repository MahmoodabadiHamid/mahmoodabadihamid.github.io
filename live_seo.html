About the Project

At the Securities and Exchange Organization of Iran, Hamid Mahmoodabadi built a real-time monitoring stack and dashboard for market-surveillance and analyst workflows. The system ingests TSETMC, TGJU, Investing.com, and internal feeds, then surfaces high-signal indicators including TEPIX, IFB, flows, YTM, FX, crypto, and commodities in a single operational view.

15-60s Core metric refresh interval
30+ Maintained time series with automatic rollups
Persian NLP Finance hashtag monitoring, symbol linking, and sentiment summaries

Market and Social Intelligence

  • Trained anomaly-detection models on order-flow features to flag suspect behavior such as sudden depth imbalances and spoof-like bursts.
  • Fed model output into live dashboards and alert channels so analysts could prioritize human review.
  • Designed a Persian-language social-feed monitor that collects finance hashtags, cleans text with Hazm, links mentions to listed symbols, and summarizes sentiment and velocity.
  • Built a lightweight RAG subsystem that retrieves recent posts and internal symbol facts for source-backed answers to market-movement questions.
